Questions, answered

Is Starter really free?+

Yes. Starter has no monthly fee and no minimum balance. You pay only for optional add-ons such as international wires ($15 each).

How does annual billing work?+

Pay yearly and get two months free — Growth becomes $41/month ($492 a year) and Scale $166/month ($1,992 a year).

Can we switch plans later?+

Any time. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated; downgrades apply at the end of the billing period.

Is Vaultline a bank?+

No — and in this case, not a real company either. Vaultline is a fictional brand created as a website design concept. No financial services are offered.
