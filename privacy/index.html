Privacy policy

Vaultline is a fictional company and this website is a design concept. This page explains, truthfully, what the site does with your data: almost nothing.

Last updated Nov 20, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The site has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels. Forms are demonstrations and never send what you type anywhere.

Cookies and storage

No cookies are set. Your browser stores two small preferences locally: your theme choice and whether you dismissed the concept banner for this session.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Third parties

No third-party scripts are loaded. Fonts and icons are self-hosted.

Contact

Questions about this concept site can go to its designer through the portfolio linked in the footer.
